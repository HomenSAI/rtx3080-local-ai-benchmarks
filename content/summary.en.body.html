Summary: local AI on RTX 3080 (10 GB, 32 GB RAM)

Version 1.13 · Test dates: 2026-09-29 – 2026-10-06

Main conclusions

  • Smartest: Ternary Bonsai-2-27B — best in general quality (85 %) and German (23/30), 40/40 in math and physics, 18/20 in code; 46.6 tok/s.
  • Best all-rounder: Qwen3.5-9B — 40/40 math and physics, 18/20 code, 262K context, 91 tok/s; the MTP variant is ×1.44 faster.
  • Images: Qwen3-VL-8B — 81 % general quality, 86.7 tok/s.
  • Fastest: MiniCPM5-2B — up to 171.6 tok/s for simple tasks and drafts.
  • Long documents: Qwen3.5-9B, Ornith-1.5-9B, MiMo-9B and Spark-X2.5-4B hold 262K tokens on the GPU.
  • Accelerators: MTP helps (×1.34–1.44); draft models slow generation down (×0.17–0.75).
  • Removed: 8 of 23 models — no stable 64K context on the GPU.
